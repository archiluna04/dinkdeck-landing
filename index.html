For hosts

Fair rotation, planned — not guessed.

Whole-queue planning across every court at once, winners-stay-on, auto-start, and a look-ahead board — so nobody's guessing who's up next, including the host.

For players

Skill-based matching that never fakes it.

Six ranks mapped to DUPR/UTPR bands, five pips per rank, and a hard rank-gap control with four settings — Dink Deck never silently starts a mismatched game.
