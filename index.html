Built for the whole night

Pick your side of the net.

Fair rotation, planned — not guessed.

Whole-queue planning across every court at once, winners-stay-on, auto-start, and a look-ahead board — so nobody's guessing who's up next, including you.

  • One-tap start — courts fill themselves from the plan
  • Sit-out fairness — games played tracked per person
  • Payment tracking — who paid, who owes, one nudge

Skill-based matching that never fakes it.

Seven ranks mapped to DUPR/UTPR bands, five pips per rank, and a hard rank-gap control with four settings — Dink Deck never silently starts a mismatched game.

  • Real record — games, win rate, streak, sessions
  • Local ladders — leaderboards by province and town
  • Find open play — browse nearby, join in a tap

Your club has a home.

A club or venue profile, a court directory with hours and rates, and a feed for what's actually happening tonight — so regulars stop asking in the group chat.

  • Post in minutes — courts, times, and who it's for
  • Members & feed — notices land where players look
  • Court directory — hours, rates, and surface for every court

Where you stand

Every game earns you a medal.

Every player carries a medal. It's the rank they're on, and how far through it they've climbed.

  • Seven ranks, five pips each — the same ladder shown throughout the app
  • Top 100 everywhere — the national board
  • Top 100 in your province and your town — read straight off where you said you play

Everything a real night needs.

Whole-queue planning

Every court planned together, not one game at a time — the look-ahead board shows the next three rounds.

Rank-gap control

Four settings decide how far apart a match may be. Mismatched games never auto-start silently.

Offline-first sync

The host device owns the queue. Results land exactly once when the connection comes back.

Court fee tracking

Split the fee, mark who paid, nudge the stragglers, attach proof of payment.

Find open play nearby

Browse clubs, courts, and events. Join in a tap, or land on the waitlist.

Ratings that update themselves

Ranks and pips move when the night closes — no spreadsheets, no arguing.
